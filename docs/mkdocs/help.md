# Help

Help with the extension comes from its repository and from the Ceedling community. Bugs go to the extension’s issues, questions to the community forum, and support contracts and training to Ceedling Assist.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-bug: **[Report a Bug][issues]**

    ---

    The extension’s GitHub issues. Check [Troubleshooting][troubleshooting] and the [Known Issues][known-issues] first, and include a debug-level log.

-   :material-forum: **[Ask the Community][forum]**

    ---

    ThrowTheSwitch’s forum, for questions about Ceedling, Unity, CMock, and this extension. [Ceedling’s documentation][ceedling-docs] covers Ceedling itself.

-   :material-handshake: **[Ceedling Assist][ceedling-assist]**

    ---

    Support contracts and training for Ceedling, from the people who make it.

-   :material-source-pull: **[Contribute][development]**

    ---

    How to build, test, and change the extension itself. [Architecture.md][architecture] explains how it works.

</div>
